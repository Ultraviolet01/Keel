## The checks

Each item was run on NEAR testnet (and the Sepolia testnets) and can be checked through the links or the commands.

1. **Sign-up connects Keel.** `keel-owner.testnet` created `nova.keel.testnet` ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/7uK1gfQUHUbyL8e3n7KWojtNdTmpgxAQtWdMGzhV2ETw)); with no other step, `near account list-keys nova.keel.testnet network-config testnet now` shows Keel's function-call key on the vault.
2. **Keel stays inside the rules.** Signing with Keel's key, a payment to a payee that is not approved was refused (`Keel Vault refused: new payee, not on the allowlist`), a proposal was accepted, and an `update_policy` call was rejected by the network (`isn't allowed by the access key`).
3. **The founder fires and rehires Keel.** `revoke_agent` deleted the key ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2pFH9SePN6REQ172fZbzRFcsiB1tvp5rFagV9PigENC5)) and Keel's next call failed; `set_agent_key` added it back ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/3XJqZ1wSxaxEMn5dd1mvfNYTmMT83uefCfvBzM2K6Vhy)).
4. **One round on a funded vault.** On `nova.keel.testnet`, Keel paid Northwind 80 alone ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Da5R9fRbMDYnRoVeyadGMD4ikgFUY8fQZir93Lb6ZQyS)), proposed Atlas 132 because Atlas was not approved ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/7vdd1dT2t3cWxUUBxSPCyG74aHwt9Q6LVp7NGupWVs2f)), refused the "supplier changed bank details" bill without signing, and proposed a 450 payroll over the cap ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/5pi9j2RtGvjn2vcUx712ytgJGkrSZyRRKeHCfosCpCS2)).
5. **No double payment after memory loss.** On `orbit.keel.testnet`, after Keel paid Northwind ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/3vcwJbZebqFq8DueC2HP1W5qsrdFwt8NgbDm5tRAD7B9)) and its memory file was deleted, the next run signed nothing, and calling `execute_payment` for the same bill failed on chain with `Keel Vault refused: this bill was already paid`.
6. **One vault address paying on four chains.** On `anchor.keel.testnet`, a 1 USDC bill on each chain was proposed, approved, signed by NEAR's MPC network and broadcast. All four receipts show a 1 USDC `Transfer` from the vault's own address `0x3d003b3d8da32261fbba854dc16188cd02436644`: [Optimism Sepolia](https://sepolia-optimism.etherscan.io/tx/0xa7312eb69bde5c12aade691af65f96e293fa291a0c3524eb2cd2b272f33b468f), [Arbitrum Sepolia](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x4864319fc6f117023ba00a343641c6f2eae02e573ef756dca5aa2ca16d9417d2), [Base Sepolia](https://sepolia.basescan.org/tx/0x4df6011a6fcc8f5896c29f5cb3e773d5f8cfcda6effc02d893c051123b3fd674), [Ethereum Sepolia](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x044b25a1517da47944215cf34177ed7c3b746a7db783799c0e9f15598c63d66e). For Base and Ethereum, Keel first reported what was missing (`has no ETH for gas`, `holds 0 USDC, needs 1 USDC; top it up`) and proposed only once the address was funded. `npm run verify` reads all four back.
7. **A larger cross-chain bill, end to end.** On `vega.keel.testnet`, a 15 USDC bill on Base Sepolia was proposed, approved ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/EyRxbDQyv4MocVD1DwhWkrgMZuWufrbRJFRxXjmHMK1V)) and paid from the vault's Base address ([transaction](https://sepolia.basescan.org/tx/0xb39efe2b80d77ced7be0bb619055c957b4271fbf490cc7a8bdd1a894a64aa1d3)); on `anchor.keel.testnet`, a 10 USDC bill on Optimism Sepolia likewise ([approval](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2Rb8SPw1cHwCdnAehxd22Hwz68zx9MGULhJnC6RWHv6U), [payment](https://sepolia-optimism.etherscan.io/tx/0xdb1cead324fe94772a9c666de29eb1a446de5de2cef38145844637d28d0bae13)).
8. **The cash buffer holds on chain.** On `ballast.keel.testnet` (4 USDC held, 3 USDC buffer), a 2 USDC payment forced through with Keel's key was refused and fully undone ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/5cCb98Z2dAe15u9WVMo8gL82YLwJNzzyU4a7bKkpdDCx)); 1 USDC, leaving exactly 3, was paid ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/3EuBM5Yo2SBLTqeuxFSgLZ81yAF6Qt6svfEieAsbeoKQ)). The hosted agent then proposed the critical payroll bill and deferred a non-critical one.
9. **Idle cash moves within its limit.** On `tideline.keel.testnet`, the hosted agent moved 1 USDC of idle cash to the founder's yield destination ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/F27nAJxn2Tp9qybek6p6Ftrg6K3RZ8Jqzvz5BGKpQz15)); a 2 USDC move forced with Keel's key was refused (`over the most Keel may move to this yield destination at once`).
10. **Yield investment at Rhea lending (formerly Burrow).** On `harvest.keel.testnet`, Keel's key deposited 20 USDC.e into the vault's own Rhea lending account at about 10.4% a year ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2gji1tfJeQLBXNKpkjbcGbEcy6QKqmzCotDyHcZgVXs8); Burrow's log shows `"event":"deposit"` for 20 USDC.e) and withdrew 5 back to the vault ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/izdqBBaYAGE325ZmgsXdMw26VeoBPM7k5WhnroBYHrd)). Then, with two 20 USDC.e bills due, 35 in cash and a 10 buffer, the agent withdrew the remaining 15 ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/EiLgEcR8LW54UCAsQRd6veLEhsReSRqquGB73jTek7EZ)) and paid both bills ([Payroll](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/joxf4BKi75EuvHfJNDn3tCeTNb27AEn31dQ4zHWHerT), [Hosting](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/H1n4cC97eLtVK8thSazeVZ8aDRh8ypbgWxpTrqfyTubR)), ending at exactly the buffer.
11. **Invoice reading.** On the hosted app, a PDF invoice was read into vendor, amount, due date, invoice number, payment address and category; a photo invoice with hidden text telling the model to raise the amount and pay another account came back with the real amount and no payment address.
12. **Attack suite.** `STRESS_VAULT=<vault> STRESS_OWNER=<owner> npm run stress` in `agent/` signs as Keel's key, the owner and an outsider and tries 53 attacks: owner methods with Keel's key, private callbacks, blocklisted, new and over-cap payees, zero, negative and maximum amounts, oversized text, the daily cap over many payments, four parallel payments of one bill, double approval, expired and replaced offers, malformed EVM transfers, pause and revoke. Result on `multi.keel.testnet`: `53 passed, 0 failed`; run again on 9 Nov against `northstar.keel.testnet` (the latest vault code, with the two-step hand-over): `53 passed, 0 failed`. The suite now restores the caps it started with when it ends, and uses the vault's own token. The web endpoints were attacked the same day, 17 attempts, all refused: notification sign-ups with no signature, someone else's signature, Keel's function-call key, a message an hour old, an email changed after signing, a signature reused for another action, a request from another website and an oversized body; round reports signed by a founder or an hour old; guessed confirm and unsubscribe tokens; and invoice uploads from another website, of the wrong type or over 3 MB. Bill names written as spreadsheet formulas (`=HYPERLINK(...)`) are exported as text, never formulas. `npm run stress:flood -- 80` filed maximum-size proposals with Keel's key until the vault stopped it at the storage reserve after 35; the owner's withdraw and pause still worked. Both need Keel's agent key.
13. **A multisig owns a vault.** `crew85369.keel.testnet` was handed to the 2-member Sputnik DAO `keelteam85369.sputnikv2.testnet` ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/wEaH3iG4ZpftBCLh15BqXcsJehLNn4njjHik2pV5Xz5)); the factory's `get_owner` and the vault's `get_policy` both name the DAO. A pause filed by one member ([proposal](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/9PLZBtr7EAdviav6ePd83W6goTv5VXgVqNY5Z6isS764)) waited after the first vote ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/6QsJmbDmkK6X1cjKHEfjazYHUoU4HbwDvxbkWaWy4xez)) and ran on the second ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/DFf77MM9dy4RfuEp4pcagjt3BBnP4qeQZ7Fxq6k59dso)); a resume went the same way ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/53Xu95VczoH3KVmLMTPG7p8ZyJ49D4K2qjGdF3nSTgaR)). With the two-step hand-over, on `deck85369.keel.testnet`: accepting with no offer, offering the vault to itself or to the factory, an outsider offering it, and an outsider or a single member accepting the multisig's offer were all refused; the owner's offer ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2DsSNSF6NXbwVKPLDerz1J5uQXXWBEKcD5cvVQTTN2gx)) was accepted by the multisig's 2-of-2 vote ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Bz6b4UbMCWa4c7ZmFhREdTFv6hUEZYjCuwRzGNGkSZGR)), after which the old owner's `pause` was refused. A 10-bill import filed as one proposal ran in full on the final vote ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/6NcFZMoUqq8zcy4XWrZ7tggRcpg84WQ3UFumieurtPyA)).
14. **Email notifications.** `keel-owner.testnet` turned notifications on from **Wallets** with a wallet-signed message and confirmed the address from the email. Pausing Compass ([transaction](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/address/compass.keel.testnet)) and sending a round report signed with Keel's agent key produced one email, "Keel · Compass: Payments paused"; resuming produced "Payments resumed"; the next round, with nothing changed, sent none. Unsigned and forged round reports are refused with `401`. The private address store is reached with Vercel's short-lived OIDC credentials, with no long-lived token in the project.
15. **Hosted agent.** Runs started every 15 minutes have completed across every vault since Nov 4; see the [run history](https://github.com/Ultraviolet01/Keel/actions/workflows/agent.yml).
